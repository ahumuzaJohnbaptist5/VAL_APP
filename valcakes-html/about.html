From a Small Kitchen to a Wholesale Empire

Val Cakes started with a simple mission: to bring joy to people's special moments through the art of baking. What began as a small home kitchen in Kabale has now grown into a full-scale bakery and wholesale supply center.

Today, under Val Investments, we not only craft beautiful custom cakes for weddings, birthdays, and anniversaries, but we also supply high-quality baking ingredients and equipment to other bakers, restaurants, and businesses across the region.
